# A Theory of Partial Automation

**Will Jobs be Commoditized or Winner-Take-All?**

Tianyu Fan (Princeton) and Pascual Restrepo (Yale)

A technology can automate some of a job's tasks but not all of them. Who gains depends
less on how much of the job is automated than on which part:

- **Core → commoditization.** When a technology takes over the tasks where skill matters
  most, pay inside the job levels out and the best workers lose their edge.
- **Periphery → winner-take-all.** When it takes over the tasks where skill matters
  least, pay spreads out and the best workers pull ahead.

This repository will hold the paper's companion website and data release: measures of
core and peripheral exposure to AI, prospective AI, and earlier automation for 923
occupations and their tasks.
